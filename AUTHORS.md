Project Authors
===============

This project was started within the Computational PhotoChemistry (CPC) group of Prof. Carolin Müller 
at the Computer Chemistry Center (CCC) of the Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)
for the purpose of standarizing post-processing, filtering and data-curation of surface-hopping
datasets produced by various tools. It was originally started by Carolin Müller and Theodor Everley 
Röhrkasten with Kevin Höllring joining during development.
